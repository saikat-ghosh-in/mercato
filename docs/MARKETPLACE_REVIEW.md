# Marketplace journey, seller fees and promotions — staging review

19 October 2026. Implemented on local `stg`; no push or changes to `main`. The existing README and `application-local.properties` edits are excluded from delivery.

## What changed

- Catalog cards now support adding/increasing/decreasing bag quantities, with wishlist additions confined to the PDP.
- Checkout has delivery details followed by combined **Review & pay**. Default addresses are selected automatically and editable there. First/only addresses become default; switching or deleting defaults is serialized per owner. PATCH preflight support fixes a real address-action failure found in Browser.
- Bag delivery methods are priced by the backend: Standard ₹50 below ₹999 merchandise subtotal, free at/above ₹999; Expedited adds ₹99; Consolidated uses Standard pricing with 1–2 extra business days. Method, estimate, fees and captured commission rates are saved on orders. Changed quotes are rejected before payment. Choices merge with a guest bag when the account bag is empty.
- Profile has a new account overview. Registration separates shopper/seller paths, confirms passwords and collects seller identity/tax/pickup information. Seller applications stay pending with shopper permissions until an email-verified applicant is explicitly reviewed by admin. Pending/rejected/approved sellers land in the seller workspace; admin accounts remain read-only in Users.
- Catalog information and Perpetual Inventory are separate. A new three-step product request covers essentials, rich information/images and review; **submission costs ₹0**. New requests and seller edits require listing approval. Admins record review notes. Unapproved/disabled listings and unavailable sellers cannot be browsed publicly, wishlisted, added to the bag or ordered. Stock updates do not grant approval.
- Initial commission is **5%**, persisted once. Admin changes require 0–20%, max two decimals, max five percentage points, a meaningful reason, exact confirmation and only one pending change. Seller in-app and email outboxes gate a minimum **60 full UTC days after all required emails are accepted**. New seller approvals extend the period. Cancellations create notices; order snapshots retain their rate.
- Admin and seller overviews now separate merchandise sales, commission, buyer platform fees, delivery collections, successful/pending refunds, unknown historical values, promotion expense and known net seller earnings. Expandable order-line calculations show the captured rate and resulting commission. Date ranges are bounded to 366 UTC days; default is 90 days. Paid-order sales follow order-creation cohorts; promotion bookings/credits follow their own movement dates.
- Seller promotions offer configurable Discovery ₹49/day, Featured ₹99/day and Spotlight ₹199/day for 3/7/14 days. Start today or within 30 UTC dates; current/scheduled campaigns are capped at three, with no overlap for the same product. Server-verified quotes, explicit earnings-deduction authorization, owner checks, request IDs and a per-seller lock protect booking. Only eligible accrued earnings can fund promotions. Cancellation credits unused whole days; started days are charged. Sponsored home/catalog shelves are labeled, eligibility checked on the server and refreshed every minute, with stale responses rejected. Higher tiers have priority; no sales or impression guarantee.

## Financial interpretation

Known net = shipped paid merchandise with known rates − refunded fulfilled merchandise − captured-rate commission − net promotion expense − unallocated refund holds. Unshipped/cancelled quantities are excluded from shipped revenue, and buyer platform fees are never deducted from seller earnings. Unknown historical rates/charge splits stay explicitly unpriced; they are not backfilled at 5%. Out-of-band refunds without reliable line attribution are conservatively held for reconciliation. Customer funds and shipping collections are not Mercato profit. Promotion bookings fund future advertising and are separate from accrued commission/platform fee income.

Campaigns are funded from a conservative **accrued earnings budget**, excluding refunded orders and unknown-rate sales, less net campaign fees. They do not charge a card, initiate a real bank transaction or represent a cash wallet. Bank payouts are not connected. Before live remittance, add tax reconciliation, verified payout onboarding, return-window reserves and an atomic remittance ledger that also reserves campaign charges; otherwise the same accrued balance could be paid out twice by a future external payout process. Reports state this limitation in the interface.

## Local startup and disposable accounts

Backend (Java 17): from `backend`, run `./mvnw.cmd spring-boot:run "-Dspring-boot.run.profiles=demo"`. Frontend: from `frontend`, run `npm run build`, then `npm run preview -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5175 --strictPort`. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5175` in Browser. The API is on 8080. `demo` uses disposable in-memory H2 and resets on restart; never enable it for production.

- Admin / existing seller: `reviewer` / `ReviewOnly!2026`.
- Shopper: `shopperreview` / `ShopOnly!2026`.
- Pending seller: `sellerreview` / `SellerOnly!2026`.

Only `demo & !prod` creates these additional accounts and a clearly synthetic paid/fulfilled order (`DEMO-EARNINGS-001`) for finance/promotion review. No payment gateway is called for this fixture. Synthetic seller PAN/GST data is not a commercial verification. The admin initializer rejects the review password outside isolated demo. Demo Cashfree is intentionally unreachable and SMTP is unconfigured; do not interpret failures as a live payment/email test.

## Schema and deployment

Back up PostgreSQL and deploy [the additive SQL script](sql/2026-10-marketplace-journey.sql) **before** the matching backend. It adds delivery/default-address fields, seller applications, fee settings/change history/outbox, listing moderation, promotion bookings and order fee/rate snapshots. Legacy defaults are normalized deterministically; the partial unique index enforces one default per owner. Existing sellers/listings remain compatible. No destructive column/type change is required. Do not seed demo accounts or enrich real product data from the fixture.

Run migrations on a staging PostgreSQL clone and use schema validation before a production rollout; this session validates JPA behavior on H2 PostgreSQL mode, not a live PostgreSQL migration. Required configuration includes verified SMTP sender and sandbox payment credentials, existing delivery/threshold/platform pricing and optional `marketplace.promotions.discovery-daily`, `featured-daily`, `spotlight-daily` properties (positive, at most ₹1,000/day, two decimals). Prices affect new quotes only. Configure notification job monitoring; SMTP acceptance is not inbox delivery and the outbox is at least once.

Rollback requires stopping new seller/listing/campaign activity first. Older APIs do not understand moderation: before serving them, disable every non-approved listing (`active=false` for PENDING/REJECTED), and reconcile/cancel outstanding campaigns. Preserve new financial, application and notice records. Do not drop additive tables as a rollback shortcut.
